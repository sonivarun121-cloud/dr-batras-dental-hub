Semi-Fixed Implant-Supported Overdentures

Semi-fixed dentures (often called snap-on or locator-retained overdentures) offer an ideal balance between the stability of implants and the convenience of removable dentures. The denture snaps securely onto dental implants, preventing slippage.

At Dr Batra's Dental Hub, we design custom-fit overdentures. The denture features specialized housings underneath that snap onto locator abutments on implants, keeping the denture firmly in place while allowing you to remove it for easy daily cleaning.

Key Treatment Benefits

  • Secure Snap-On Fit: Dentures stay firmly in place when talking and eating, with zero slippage.
  • Removable for Hygiene: Snaps off easily, allowing for thorough daily cleaning and oral care.
  • Fewer Implants Needed: Typically requires only 2 to 4 implants per arch, making it a cost-effective option.
  • No Messy Adhesives: Eliminates the need for denture creams, pastes, or powders.

Semi Fixed Dentures FAQs

1. How do snap-on overdentures work?

Dental implants are placed in the jaw, and locator abutments are attached. The denture is customized with matching snap sockets underneath that lock onto these abutments.

2. Can I sleep with my semi-fixed dentures?

We recommend removing them at night to clean them and to allow your gum tissues to rest, which helps maintain excellent oral health.

3. How long do the locator snaps last?

The small nylon caps inside the denture that snap onto the locators experience wear over time and are usually replaced every 1 to 2 years in a brief office visit.

Semi-Fixed Dentures — What to Expect

How do they work? Semi-fixed dentures snap securely onto two or more dental implants surgically placed in your jawbone. Compared to traditional removable dentures, they offer vastly improved stability and chewing force. Compared to fully fixed options, they are more affordable and easier to clean. The process involves implant placement, a healing period, and the customized crafting of your overdenture. We provide detailed maintenance instructions to ensure the longevity of your new smile.
