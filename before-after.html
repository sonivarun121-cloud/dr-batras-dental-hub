    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. Are the before-and-after results real?

    Yes, all before-and-after photos show actual patients treated at Dr Batra's Dental Hub with their informed consent.

    2. What treatments show the most dramatic results?

    Smile makeovers, dental implants for missing teeth, teeth whitening, and orthodontic treatments typically show the most visible transformations.

    3. Can I get similar results?

    Results depend on your individual oral condition. Book a consultation at Dr Batra's Dental Hub for a personalized assessment and treatment plan.

    4. How long does it take to see results?

    Some treatments like teeth whitening show instant results. Others like braces or implants take weeks to months for full transformation.
