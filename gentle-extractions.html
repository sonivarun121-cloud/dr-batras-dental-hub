Atraumatic Bone-Preserving Tooth Removals

While our primary goal is to preserve natural teeth, there are situations where a tooth is too heavily fractured, infected, or decayed to be saved. In these cases, extraction is necessary to protect your jaw from spreading dental infection.

At Dr Batra's Dental Hub, we perform extractions with high comfort using modern, atraumatic methods. Instead of using force, we utilize specialized micro-luxators to gently ease the tooth out, fully preserving the surrounding jawbone. This preservation is critical for supporting a dental implant later.

  • Jawbone Socket Preservation: Keeping the bone intact for immediate or future implant placement.

Tooth Extraction FAQs

1. Is tooth extraction painful?

Not at all. We utilize powerful localized anesthetics to fully numb the tooth and surrounding gums. You will feel a sensation of pressure as the tooth is gently loosened, but no sharp pain or discomfort.

2. What is an 'atraumatic' extraction?

Atraumatic extraction is a advanced technique that utilizes micro-luxators to gently slide the tooth out of its socket rather than pulling it. This preserves the surrounding socket bone, which is critical if you plan to get a dental implant later.

3. How can I prevent dry socket after an extraction?

Dry socket happens when the blood clot in the extraction socket is dislodged. You can prevent this by avoiding spitting, sucking through a straw, rinsing vigorously, or smoking for at least 48 to 72 hours after the procedure.
