Frequently Asked Questions

1. What sterilization protocols does Dr Batra's Dental Hub follow?

We follow WHO and Indian Dental Association guidelines with Class B autoclaving, UV sterilization, chemical disinfection, and single-use disposable materials.

2. Is the dental clinic safe from infections?

Absolutely. Our multi-step sterilization protocol eliminates 100% of bacteria, viruses, and spores. Every instrument is sterilized before use.

3. Do you use disposable materials?

Yes, all needles, suction tips, gloves, masks, and patient bibs are single-use disposable items that are safely discarded after each patient.

4. Can I see the sterilization process?

Yes, we maintain full transparency. Our sterilization area is visible, and we're happy to show patients our protocols upon request.

Advanced Barrier Controls

Our 5-Step Surgical Sterilization Protocol

We leave absolutely zero room for pathogens. Here is exactly how we ensure 100% sterile execution for every clinical consultation and surgery.

01

Enzymatic Pre-Soaking

Instruments are immediately immersed in a medical-grade enzymatic solution to break down organic proteins and completely deactivate contaminants.

02

Ultrasonic Cleansing

Instruments undergo high-frequency ultrasonic micro-cavitation. This removes microscopic particles from hinges and textured surfaces that manual scrubbing misses.

03

Hermetic Sealing

Dried instruments are sealed individually in medical surgical pouches with dual internal and external chemical color indicators to verify sterility.

04

Autoclaving

Pouches are sterilized in a fractionated vacuum autoclave at 134°C under high pressure, completely destroying all bacteria, viruses, and heat-resistant spores.

05

Aseptic Unsealing

Sealed sterile cassettes are stored in clean cabinets and only opened directly in front of the patient inside the operatory. This protects aseptic integrity right up to the moment of use.

Operatory Environmental Controls

Water & Air Environmental Cleansing

Apart from surgical clinical instruments, we strictly purify the operational environment inside our treatment rooms:

  • Medical Air Filtration: Operating rooms use high-efficiency particulate air (HEPA) filters combined with UV-C air sanitizers to continuously eliminate airborne droplets, micro-dust, and pathogens.
  • Dual Purified Waterlines: All dental operatory units are fed with multi-stage purified reverse-osmosis (RO) water to prevent biofilm build-up in clinical water syringe lines.
  • Operatory Disinfection: Between patients, all surfaces, dental chairs, and lights are sanitarily wiped down with WHO-approved rapid surface disinfectants.

Clinic Sterilization — What to Expect

Our comprehensive sterilization process begins with the rigorous cleaning of all instruments, followed by packaging and sterilization in a Class B autoclave, which uses pressurized steam to eliminate all microorganisms. We use chemical indicators to verify that sterilization has been successful. Treatment rooms are thoroughly disinfected between patients, and high-touch surfaces are wrapped in barrier films.
