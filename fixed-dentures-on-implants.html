Fixed Dentures on Implants (All-on-4 / All-on-6)

For patients missing an entire arch of teeth, implant-supported fixed dentures represent the absolute gold standard of restoration. By placing titanium implant roots directly into the jaw, we provide a permanent anchor for a custom-crafted bridge.

At Dr Batra's Dental Hub, chief implant surgeon Dr. Gursimran Singh Batra utilizes state-of-the-art All-on-4 and All-on-6 surgical protocols. This technique allows us to securely restore a full set of teeth with maximum stability, comfort, and aesthetics.

Key Treatment Benefits

  • Maximum Stability: Anchored by 4 to 6 titanium implants for complete structural load-bearing.
  • Bone Preservation: Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the facial collapse associated with tooth loss.
  • Natural Aesthetics: Bespoke Zirconia or hybrid bridges that look indistinguishable from natural teeth.
  • High Comfort: No bulky acrylic plate covering the roof of your mouth, keeping your sense of taste natural.

Fixed Dentures on Implants FAQs

1. What is the All-on-4 or All-on-6 protocol?

It is an advanced surgical technique where 4 or 6 dental implants are strategically placed in the jaw to support a full-arch fixed bridge, replacing all missing teeth.

2. Are implant-supported dentures permanent?

Yes. The bridge is securely screwed onto the implants by the dentist. It is highly durable and designed to last for decades with excellent hygiene.

3. Is the implant surgery painful?

No. The surgery is performed under localized anesthesia, and we offer advanced pain-management protocols. Most patients experience only mild, manageable soreness for a few days post-op.

Fixed Dentures on Implants — What to Expect

We specialize in both All-on-4 and All-on-6 implant systems. The procedure timeline begins with a thorough 3D scan to assess your bone volume and candidacy. During surgery, 4 to 6 titanium implants are strategically placed in the jaw. In many cases, a temporary fixed bridge is attached the very same day. After a healing period where the implants fuse with the bone, your final, highly durable and natural-looking fixed denture is permanently attached.
