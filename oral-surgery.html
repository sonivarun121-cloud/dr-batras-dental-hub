Complex Surgical Dental Procedures

Minor oral surgery covers a range of specialized surgical procedures aimed at resolving complex dental conditions, correcting anatomical bone structures, or preparing jaws for prosthetic rehabilitations.

At Dr Batra's Dental Hub, our clinical surgical procedures follow strict sterilization protocols to eliminate infection risks. From impacted wisdom tooth extractions to jaw bone grafting, we provide high-safety, sterile clinical surgical care.

Our Oral Surgery Procedures

  • Impacted Wisdom Teeth Extraction: Safe surgical removal of third molars that are trapped beneath gums or bone.
  • Jawbone Grafting: Adding bone-graft materials to restore jaw volume for future dental implants.
  • Alveoloplasty (Jaw Reshaping): Pre-prosthetic surgical smoothing of the jaw bone ridge to ensure dentures fit comfortably.

Oral Surgery FAQs

1. Is wisdom tooth surgery painful?

The procedure is performed under complete localized anesthesia. You will feel pressure but no pain during the extraction. Post-operative discomfort is standard and is easily managed with prescribed anti-inflammatory medications.

2. What is jaw bone grafting and why is it needed?

Bone grafting is a minor surgical procedure where bone material is added to areas of the jaw where bone has been lost. It is typically required to rebuild the supporting jaw ridge to safely support a dental implant.

3. What is the typical recovery time after oral surgery?

Most patients recover within 3 to 5 days after minor oral surgeries (like wisdom tooth removals). We provide a detailed post-operative care checklist, including eating soft foods and avoiding straws, to ensure fast, complications-free healing.
