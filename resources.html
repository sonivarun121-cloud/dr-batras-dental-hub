Clinical Guidelines

Post-Treatment Recovery Guides

To ensure the longevity and successful healing of your dental treatments, please read and follow these specialized post-care guidelines.

Dental Implants Recovery

To ensure successful osseointegration of your bio-compatible titanium implants, adhere strictly to these clinical instructions:

  • Maintain a soft diet (soups, smoothies, yogurt) for the first 48 hours. Avoid hot liquids.
  • Apply a cold compress on the cheek for 10-minute intervals to reduce mild facial swelling.
  • Brush gently around the surgical site; do not spit forcefully or touch the site with your fingers.
  • Take prescribed pain relievers and antibiotics exactly as instructed by Dr. Batra.

Root Canal Treatment Care

A root canal eliminates painful infections. Follow these instructions to protect the temporary filling and facilitate tooth healing:

  • Do not chew on the treated side until the permanent aesthetic crown is fully cemented.
  • Avoid hard, crunchy, or sticky foods that might dislodge the temporary sealing material.
  • Expect mild sensitivity for 2-3 days; manage discomfort with prescribed anti-inflammatories.
  • Maintain standard brushing and flossing routines to keep the surrounding gum area pristine.

Surgical Extraction Post-Care

Following a wisdom tooth or surgical extraction, protecting the blood clot is vital to prevent dry sockets and accelerate healing:

  • Keep biting firmly on the sterile gauze pack for 45 minutes to encourage stable clotting.
  • Strictly avoid drinking through straws, smoking, or spitting forcefully for the first 24 hours.
  • Do not drink carbonated or extremely hot beverages. Consume cool, soft meals instead.
  • Rinse very gently with lukewarm salt water starting 24 hours after your extraction.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What post-treatment care instructions should I follow?

Follow the specific care instructions provided by your dentist. General tips include avoiding hard foods, maintaining oral hygiene, and attending follow-up appointments.

2. How do I care for dental implants?

Clean around implants with a soft toothbrush, use interdental brushes, avoid chewing ice or hard objects, and visit your dentist every 6 months.

3. What should I eat after dental procedures?

Stick to soft foods like yogurt, mashed potatoes, and soups for the first few days. Avoid hot, spicy, and crunchy foods until your dentist advises otherwise.

4. When should I call the dentist after a procedure?

Contact us immediately if you experience severe pain, excessive bleeding, swelling that worsens after 48 hours, or signs of infection like fever.
