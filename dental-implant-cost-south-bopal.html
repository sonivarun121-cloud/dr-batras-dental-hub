Dental Implant Cost in South Bopal & Ahmedabad — Complete Price Guide

HomeDental Implant Cost in South Bopal & Ahmedabad

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth, offering unparalleled durability, functionality, and aesthetics. However, one of the most common questions patients have is regarding the cost of dental implants in South Bopal and Ahmedabad. At Dr Batra's Dental Hub, we believe in complete transparency to help you make an informed decision about your oral health investment.

Factors Influencing Dental Implant Costs

The total price of a dental implant procedure isn't a simple flat rate. It varies based on several crucial factors:

  • Brand of Implant: We use top-tier global brands. Premium brands like Nobel Biocare (Swedish) and Straumann (Swiss) generally cost more due to their extensive research and proven long-term success rates compared to high-quality but more economical Korean brands (like Osstem or Dentium).
  • Number of Implants: Whether you need a single tooth replacement, multiple implants, or a full mouth rehabilitation (All-on-4 or All-on-6).
  • Need for Additional Procedures: If you have experienced bone loss, you may require a bone grafting procedure or a sinus lift before the implant can be placed, which adds to the overall cost.
  • Type of Crown: The visible part of the tooth (the crown) attached to the implant can be made of different materials, such as Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal (PFM) or Zirconia. Zirconia crowns are more aesthetic and durable, thus slightly more expensive.

Estimated Pricing Guide at Dr Batra's Dental Hub

Please note: These are estimated base prices. A precise quote can only be provided after a clinical examination and 3D CBCT scan.

  • Single Dental Implant (Korean Brand): Typically starts from ₹20,000 to ₹25,000 (excluding crown).
  • Single Dental Implant (Premium Swiss/Swedish Brand): Usually ranges from ₹35,000 to ₹45,000+ (excluding crown).
  • Implant Crowns: PFM crowns are more affordable, while high-grade Zirconia crowns can add ₹8,000 to ₹15,000 to the total cost.
  • Full Mouth Implants (All-on-4 / All-on-6): For completely edentulous patients, full mouth solutions can range significantly based on the technique and materials chosen, generally starting from ₹1.5 Lakhs to ₹3 Lakhs per arch.

Why Invest in Dental Implants?

While the initial cost may seem higher than alternatives like dentures or bridges, dental implants are a lifelong investment. They prevent jawbone deterioration, do not require altering adjacent healthy teeth, and function exactly like natural teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Most standard health insurance policies in India do not cover dental implants as they are often classified as cosmetic procedures. However, specific dental insurance plans or corporate policies might offer partial coverage.

2. Do you offer financing or EMI options for implants?

We understand that implants are a significant investment. Please speak with our front desk during your consultation to discuss available payment plans or financing options.

3. Which is better, a Korean implant or a Swiss implant?

Both are excellent choices. Swiss/Swedish implants have decades of clinical research behind them and are often preferred in complex cases. Korean implants are highly reliable, offer great success rates, and are more cost-effective.

4. How long does a dental implant last?

With proper oral hygiene and regular dental checkups, a high-quality dental implant can last a lifetime.

5. Is the implant procedure painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you will not feel pain during the surgery. Post-operative discomfort is usually mild and easily managed with prescribed medication.
