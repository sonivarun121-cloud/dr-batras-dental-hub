Comfort & Technology

Modern, Ergonomic
Treatment Operatories

We believe that premium dental care should be defined by both clinical precision and comforting luxury. Our operatory suites are designed with state-of-the-art patient chairs and diagnostics to support a calm, comfortable experience.

Ergonomic Comfort: Premium super-cushioned memory foam chair with dual-axis head support, engineered to eliminate pressure points during longer appointments.

Intraoral Screenings: Real-time high-definition camera displays on chairside LED screens, allowing you to see exactly what the doctor sees for complete transparency.

Clean-Water Systems: Independent waterline systems equipped with auto-disinfecting blocks to ensure 100% pathogen-free water for all procedures.

Hygiene & Safety

State-of-the-Art Sterilization
and Disinfection

At Dr Batra's Dental Hub, patient safety is our absolute priority. We operate a dedicated, hospital-grade Sterilization Zone equipped with advanced digital autoclaves and strict contamination barriers to ensure 100% sterile clinical operations.

Autoclave Sterilization

Utilizing fractionated vacuum autoclaving to eliminate all bacterial spores, viruses, and pathogens from clinical tools.

Segregated Bio-waste

Color-coded segregation bins compliant with WHO standards, ensuring safe disposal and zero cross-infection risks.

Needle & Sharp Safety

Immediate electric needle destruction and safe sharps management to prevent injury and maintain complete clinical hygiene.

Frequently Asked Questions

2. Where is Dr Batra's Dental Hub located in South Bopal?

Dr Batra's Dental Hub is located at B-17/C, Sun South Street, Behind Safal Parisar-1, South Bopal, Ahmedabad 380057. We are easily accessible from Bopal, Shela, Ghuma, Shilaj, and SP Ring Road.
