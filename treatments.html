Clinical FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are treatments at Dr Batra's Dental Hub pain-free?

Yes! We employ computerized localized anesthesia, micro-thin needles, and modern pain-management guidelines to ensure that all treatments (including Root Canals and Implants) are completely comfortable and virtually pain-free.

What are the clinical benefits of Invisible Aligners over traditional braces?

Invisible aligners are custom-made medical grade polyurethane trays that are virtually transparent. Unlike metal wires, they are removable during meals, highly hygienic, cause no oral abrasions, and dynamically align teeth with extreme precision.

How long do custom dental implants last?

With premium medical-grade titanium roots and Dr. Batra's expert surgical mapping, dental implants can easily last a lifetime. Excellent daily brushing, flossing, and semi-annual scaling checkups will preserve the healthy supporting jaw bone indefinitely.

How many visits does a Root Canal Treatment require?

With our advanced micro-rotary instrumentation and digital apex locators, most standard root canal treatments can be comfortably completed in a single sitting of 45-60 minutes. Crown fitting is typically completed in a subsequent brief visit.
