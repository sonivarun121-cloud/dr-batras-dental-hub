Artistic Teeth Reshaping & Cosmetic Bonding

Minor dental imperfections, such as tiny chips, sharp edges, uneven lengths, or slight overlaps, can distract from an otherwise beautiful smile. Teeth reshaping (enameloplasty) and cosmetic bonding offer fast, painless, and highly conservative solutions.

At Dr Batra's Dental Hub, we perform precise contouring by gently smoothing away small amounts of enamel or adding tooth-colored composite resins to rebuild chipped areas, restoring a harmonious alignment in a single, comfortable visit.

Key Treatment Benefits

  • Immediate Results: Reshaping and cosmetic bonding are completed in a single visit.
  • Atraumatic & Painless: The procedure is completely non-invasive and does not require local anesthesia.
  • Enamel Preservation: Only minimal, safe contouring of superficial enamel is performed.
  • Seamless Bonding: High-strength composite materials are shade-matched to blend invisibly with your natural teeth.

Teeth Reshaping FAQs

1. Does teeth reshaping hurt?

No. Since teeth reshaping only involves polishing the outer, non-sensitive enamel layer, the procedure is typically painless and usually requires no numbing or injections.

2. How long does composite bonding last?

Cosmetic bonding material typically lasts 3 to 7 years. It can be easily polished, repaired, or touched up over time as needed.

3. Can reshaping fix crooked teeth?

Reshaping can correct minor overlaps, uneven heights, and rough borders. For moderate to severe misalignment, we recommend orthodontic treatments like clear aligners.

Teeth Reshaping — What to Expect

The contouring process involves carefully removing tiny amounts of tooth enamel to alter the shape, length, or surface of the tooth. Ideal candidates for this procedure are those with minor cosmetic issues and healthy teeth. Because enamel has no nerves, the procedure is entirely painless and usually requires no anesthesia. After reshaping, we meticulously polish the teeth for a smooth, natural finish, instantly elevating your smile's harmony.
