Dentures & Bridges FAQs

1. What is the difference between a dental bridge and a denture?

A dental bridge is a fixed prosthesis that is cemented onto adjacent teeth and cannot be removed by the patient. Dentures are removable prosthetic appliances that replace some or all missing teeth.

2. How long does it take to get used to a new denture?

It typically takes a few weeks to adjust to speaking and eating with a new denture. Dr. Batra will perform follow-up adjustments to ensure a comfortable, sore-free fit.

3. How do I care for my dental bridge?

Maintain standard brushing and flossing, using specialized floss threaders or interdental brushes to clean beneath the artificial suspended bridge tooth.
