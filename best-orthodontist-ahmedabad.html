Comprehensive Orthodontic Solutions

We understand that every smile is unique. Dr Batra offers a variety of orthodontic treatments tailored to suit your lifestyle, aesthetic preferences, and budget:

  • Traditional Metal Braces: The most reliable and cost-effective method for correcting severe crowding and bite issues. Modern metal braces are smaller and more comfortable than ever before.
  • Ceramic (Clear) Braces: These function like metal braces but use tooth-colored brackets, making them much less noticeable. They are a popular choice among teenagers and adults.
  • Clear Aligners (Invisalign): The ultimate in discreet orthodontics. These custom-made, virtually invisible plastic trays gently shift your teeth into place. They are removable, allowing you to eat and clean your teeth with ease.
  • Growth Modulation & Early Orthodontics: Early intervention for children to guide jaw growth and prevent complex issues later in life.

The Treatment Journey and Expectations

Your orthodontic journey begins with a comprehensive consultation, including digital scans and X-rays. Dr Japneet will discuss the best options for your specific case and outline the expected treatment timeline, which typically ranges from 12 to 24 months depending on the complexity.

Regular follow-up visits (usually every 4-6 weeks) are essential to monitor progress and make necessary adjustments. After the active treatment phase, custom retainers will be provided to ensure your new smile stays perfect for a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the right age to first visit an orthodontist?

The Indian Orthodontic Society recommends an initial orthodontic evaluation by age 7 to identify any developing issues early.

2. Am I too old for braces?

No! Orthodontic treatment is highly effective for adults as long as your gums and bone structure are healthy. We offer discreet options like ceramic braces and aligners perfect for adults.

3. How much do braces cost in Ahmedabad?

The cost varies widely based on the type of braces chosen and the complexity of the case. Traditional metal braces are generally the most affordable, while clear aligners are a premium option.

4. Do braces hurt?

You may experience some mild soreness for a few days after braces are initially placed or adjusted, but it is generally manageable and temporary.

5. Are clear aligners as effective as regular braces?

Yes, for most mild to moderate alignment issues, clear aligners are highly effective. However, very complex bite problems might still require traditional braces for optimal results.
